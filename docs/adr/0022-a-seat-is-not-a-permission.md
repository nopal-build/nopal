# ADR-022 — A seat is not a permission.

**Status:** Accepted, 2026-09-23

**Context.** The dashboard splits by reader: a guide sees across their projects, a client sees their own project and nothing else, and an observer (leadership, or a guide not running this project) looks in without being asked anything. A Steep-o-meter reading from a client reaches the project's guides and observers and nobody else. Projects already had a per-person role, the Sharing Role (Owner, Crafter, Observer), stored as `sharing: [{human, role}]` in the project's README front matter. That role is a permission: `is_owner` decides who may write. Clients were given the least-privileged role, Observer, when legacy shares were backfilled, so "Observer" already meant "a client" in practice and "a leader looking in" in the dashboard guide.

**Decision.** Where a person sits on a project is a second field on the same sharing entry, `seat: guide | client | observer`, independent of `role`. The project's creator is always a guide. Anyone else with no seat sits where their sharing role already puts them: an owner-tier role (Owner, Crafter) reads as a guide, any other role as a client. A default never grants more than the person's role already does (Austin, 2026-09-24): the team keeps working as before, and legacy clients, backfilled to Observer, read as clients. What the dashboard returns is decided by the seat, on the server, in `buildDashboard` (`dashboard.server.ts`): a client row carries the page's read and never the guides' ask or anyone's reading; a guide or observer row carries the ask and the clients' readings; an observer does not set the meter. A reshare that names only roles keeps each person's seat (`keepExistingSeats`). Steep readings live in their own table (`steep_readings`), never in `file_refs`, marks or the graph, so a reading cannot reach a page a client reads.

**Why it looks removable.** Two role-ish fields on one entry looks like duplication; folding "client" into the Sharing Roles as a fourth role looks tidier. It is not: a client may need to write (Crafter) and a leader may hold Owner, and the moment the screen reads the permission to decide the posture, giving a client write access turns them into a guide and shows them every reading on the project. Readings stored as marks or Cards look like reuse of the annotation pipeline; they would become nodes and then words on the Efforts page, which clients read.

**How you'd know.** A client's dashboard shows another project's name, an ask written for the guides, or another person's Steep reading. A client who was given Crafter to upload photos starts seeing the guide view. A CLI reshare quietly resets every seat to guide. A Steep reading appears as a line on a project page.

**Test.** `webapp/app/tests/graphlog/dashboard.test.ts` (only your projects; readings reach guides and observers only; a reshare keeps seats; the store and tap route send nothing) and `fruits/app/tests/dashboardRender.test.tsx` (the meter and log box on top for every seat; a steep note has no alarm; a client sees no counts).
